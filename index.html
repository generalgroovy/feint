Read the duel

You both act on each turn: movement first, then attacks. Strike reaches only the next tile and deals two damage. If both strike in range, both take damage—even on a final hit.

Guard counters a strike for one damage, but using it tires your guard. Tired Guard is unavailable; Step or Strike restores it. Their guard always counters your strike. Step moves toward them when far away, or away when adjacent; the button says which. At the left edge, Hold at edge stays put and restores Guard; it cannot dodge an incoming strike.

Their next move is exact, not a guess. It stays visible so you can work out a reply. There is no timer. Undo is unlimited; changing rival or reloading starts a fresh duel. Three rivals use different repeating patterns.
